# Feature: Improve Website SEO Performance

## Goal
Enhance the website’s visibility in search engine results by implementing key on-page SEO and technical SEO best practices, and establishing measurable progress tracking over six months.

## Why this matters
Improved SEO increases organic traffic, enhances user experience, and contributes to brand visibility, making the website more discoverable to potential users and better aligned with modern search engine algorithms.

## Rough shape
- Conduct an SEO audit to evaluate current on-page and technical SEO performance  
- Identify key areas for improvement (e.g., meta tags, headers, URL structure, site speed, mobile responsiveness)  
- Optimize underperforming pages with relevant keywords and quality content  
- Fix crawl errors, internal linking issues, and structured data opportunities  
- Set up monitoring and analytics tools to track improvement goals  
- Establish a six-month timeline for evaluating SEO impact with defined KPIs  

## Out of scope for this feature
- Paid search/PPC campaign optimization  
- Social media meta tags beyond basic Open Graph  
- Third-party content partnerships or external link-building efforts  
- Redesigning the overall site architecture unless directly tied to critical SEO issues  
- Deep content creation or rewriting beyond optimization guidelines  
- Advanced local SEO or specific international SEO configurations
