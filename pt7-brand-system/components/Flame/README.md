# Flame

The 1928 Olympic flame from the Marathon Tower, drawn as three slanted blades on a bowl. It is a STORY illustration — never part of the logo and never locked up with "PT7".

- Use it where the story is told: hero art (on the tower), the venue/history section, campaign posters, merch graphics, the map pin.
- Full colour: centre blade `flame-core`, sides `flame-edge`, bowl `ink`, 5px ground-coloured gaps. One colour: all `gold-text`, or black on gold.
- Never inside or next to the logo as if it were one mark; keep at least the logo's height between them.
- Never on a gold fill in full colour.
