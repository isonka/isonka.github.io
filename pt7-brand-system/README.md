# PT7

PT7 is a Pilates, yoga and strength studio at the Olympisch Stadion, Amsterdam. The brand is calm, precise and premium — the confidence of a coach who knows your name, not the noise of a gym. Cream space, light type, one gold accent, and a thin-line logo that everything else is drawn to match.

**Family:** PT7 (studio) · PT7 Academy (instructor training) · PT7 Yoga. Future lines (Strength, Recovery, Coffee) use the same lockup. Locations: "PT7 Stadion", "PT7 Zuid", same logo.

## Principles

1. **Match the logo.** The PT7 mark is thin and light. Type, icons and illustration are drawn at the same weight: light headlines, hairlines, line art.
2. **Calm, not quiet.** Lots of cream space; energy comes from one gold accent per view and the 7-stripe — never from size or shouting.
3. **Seven lanes.** The 7 in PT7 is the organising idea: seven ways to train (Reformer, Strength, Yoga, Mat Pilates, TRX & cardio, Pre- & postnatal, Private).
4. **People first.** Real coaches, real photos, real reviews.

## Voice

Warm, precise, short. A coach who explains, not a drill sergeant.

- Do: "Find your lane." · "Seven ways to train, one coach beside you." · "New here? Start with three." · "Meet your coaches."
- Do: numbers that reassure — "up to ten per class", "45 minutes".
- Don't: all caps, exclamation marks, gym slang ("crush", "grind", "beast"), spa clichés ("indulge", "pamper", "journey").
- Sentence case everywhere: headlines, buttons, navigation.

## Colour

Cream (`ground`) is the page. White (`ground-raised`) for cards and alternating sections, stone (`ground-stone`) for quiet bands. Charcoal (Evening theme) for one feature section per page — the Academy.

- **Gold** is a fill: primary buttons, lane 7, the 7-stripe. Text on gold is `on-accent`. Gold-coloured text uses `gold-text`.
- **Discipline accents** appear only in the 7-stripe: `gold` Pilates, `strength` orange, `yoga` green, `ink` Private.
- Roughly 75% cream/white, 20% ink, 5% gold.

## Type

One family: **Poppins** (already pt7.nl's face, and close to the logo's lettering).

- Headlines: Light (300), sentence case, tight tracking, with the key word in Medium (500): "Find your **lane.**"
- Eyebrow line above headlines in `gold-text`, Medium, sentence case.
- Body Regular (400); buttons Medium (500).
- No condensed faces, no italics for emphasis, no all caps.
- Google Fonts: `Poppins:wght@300;400;500;600`.

## Shapes

- **The 7-stripe:** a narrow gold bar slanted like the 7 in the logo (clip-path `polygon(24px 0, 40px 0, 14px 100%, 0 100%)` at 40px wide, or the small 22px version beside names). Used on the lane panel edge and next to coach names, coloured by discipline. One per element.
- **Track line art:** seven thin concentric lanes with lane 7 in solid gold — the hero illustration. Hairline strokes in `line`, numbers in Poppins Light.
- Square corners, hairline borders, no shadows, no gradients.

## Logo and lockups

**The PT7 logo is the only mark.** Thin-line "PT" in `ink`, the 7 in gold (`gold-deep` on cream for contrast, `gold` on charcoal). Sub-brands add their word under "PT" in Poppins. Circle badge for avatars. See Lockup.

## The location story (Stadion only)

The Olympisch Stadion was built for the 1928 Games; the first Olympic flame burned in a bowl on its Marathon Tower. PT7 trains in unit 15, in the upper-left corner.

- **Where it lives:** the venue section, "how to find us", opening posts and press only. "Where the first flame burned." · "Unit 15, upper-left corner of the Stadion."
- **Never a brand headline**, never in the logo. The flame illustration (see Flame) is a story element; on the website it is told in text and a line map.
- Avoid "Olympic" in ads and product names; stating the history is fine.

## Photography

Natural light, real classes, the coach's hands correcting form, the stadium brick outside. Full colour, warm and calm. Crop people to 4:5 portraits with square corners. One consistent shoot of all coaches beats eight mismatched headshots.
